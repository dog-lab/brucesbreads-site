## The Story Behind the Ingredients

Banana peppers belong to the same species, *Capsicum annuum*, as bell peppers and jalapenos -- a lineage that traces back to the domestication of chili peppers in Mesoamerica thousands of years ago. The banana pepper itself, as a distinct mild, tapered cultivar, is closely tied to 20th-century American agriculture and became a fixture of pizzerias, delis, and sub shops.

Provolone traces its roots to Southern Italy, particularly the Campania region, where it belongs to the *pasta filata* (stretched-curd) family of cheeses alongside mozzarella.

## Troubleshooting / FAQ

**Cheese moat around the base of the loaf** -- Provolone wasn't cold enough going in. Keep cheese chilled right up until you fold it in.

**Dough feels slack or gummy after folding in inclusions** -- Onions or peppers weren't drained/blotted dry enough. Pat both thoroughly dry before folding in.

## Bloopers

Even a well-tested recipe goes sideways sometimes. The cheese moat: a ring of melted provolone pools and crisps around the base of the loaf instead of staying tucked inside. The fix -- keep those cubes in the fridge right up until the moment you fold them in.
